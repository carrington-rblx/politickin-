WHO WE ARE

POLITICS IS
EVERYONE'S BUSINESS.

Politickin' is an independent platform built to make politics more accessible, bring people into the conversation, and help communities hold the people in power accountable.

OUR PURPOSE

INFORMED COMMUNITIES
ARE STRONGER COMMUNITIES.

Political decisions shape everyday life — from the services people rely on to the rights and opportunities that shape their futures. Understanding those decisions shouldn't feel out of reach. Politickin' aims to make the issues, the people, and the stakes easier to follow so more people can take part in the conversation.

01 / ACCESS

Make it understandable.

Present political issues in a clear, approachable way so readers can better understand what is happening and why it matters.

02 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Ask the hard questions.

Encourage scrutiny of public decisions and keep attention on the people entrusted with representing their communities.

03 / PARTICIPATION

Bring more voices in.

Make room for people to learn, share perspectives, and engage with the issues that affect them.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PLATFORM

BUILT TO GET PEOPLE TALKING.

Politickin' was created by 4S1MOLT with a simple idea: politics should be easier to understand and open to everyone. The goal is to build a place where people can stay informed, exchange ideas, and pay closer attention to the decisions shaping their future.
