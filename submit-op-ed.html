Submit an Op-Ed to POLITICKIN'

We are always looking for elected officials, candidates, party members, and other thought leaders to write thought-provoking op-eds about the challenges and opportunities that Americans are facing.

Specifically, we are looking to amplify voices on topics like:

  • Threats to our rights and freedoms
  • Threats to our democracy
  • Opposing authoritarianism and oligarchy
  • Explaining key things that are happening internally in parties
  • Exploring potential failures of the political establishment
  • Reflections on and insights into how the Progressive Party can move forward in the first Wadgerbadger era
  • Calling out dangerous extremism, bad actors, and bad actions in the political sphere that people may be unaware of.

What we're looking for from an op-ed

Things we like:

  • Concise and focused pieces between 600 and 1,000 words
  • Relevant and timely content
  • A clear, structured argument or main point
  • A unique perspective
  • An eye toward explaining how this specific argument has implications on the american people

Things we don't like:

  • Lack of structure or focus
  • Overly broad content (i.e. discussing lots of different issues)
  • Niche content (i.e. focusing solely on one piece of legislation or a hyperlocal issue)
  • Self-promotional pieces
  • Intentionally inflammatory, libelous, or discriminatory language
